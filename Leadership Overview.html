Metric definitions (proposed — confirm with Jake)
Operating week
Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 ET. Trends compare with the prior week.
Filters
Recruitment numbers belong to recruiters; hours and units collected belong to the collection team. When the filter is set to one type or person, sections for the other type show the region total and are tagged "Region total".
Recruiting
Registrations vs. the sum of recruiter weekly targets. On track ≥ 100%, Watch 80–99%, Needs attention below 80%.
Pipeline
Dogs approved by the medical director ÷ first appointments scheduled in the week. Target 70%.
First-time donor show rate
First appointments attended ÷ scheduled. Target 85%. Donation-day show rate is tracked separately under Donation days.
Travel efficiency
Recruiting miles per registration. Target 25 or fewer.
Donor retention
Share of Pending, Approved, Active and Lab work dogs not at Action, Critical or Past 95 days. Target 90%.
Risk tiers
Archive track (Pending, clock from registration): Watch 30–59, Action 60–79, Critical 80–95 days. Retire track (Approved from screening date; Active and Lab work from last donation): Watch 57–70, Action 71–84, Critical 85–95. Paused dogs and dogs scheduled before their deadline are excluded.
Needs attention
Generated from the same thresholds: what is off target, where, the likely driver, and who acts.
