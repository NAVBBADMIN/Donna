Definitions

Review week
Monday to Sunday, Eastern time. The meeting reviews the selected week and plans the next one.
Goals
Set by managers on the Recruiter Goals page.
First-time donor show rate
First appointments attended, divided by first appointments scheduled.
Approved
Screened and approved by the medical director, with no donation yet. The retire clock runs from the screening date.
Urgent
Dogs past day 95 or within 10 days of retirement or archive, and overdue tasks.
Retire track
Approved, Active and Lab work dogs. Watch 57 to 70 days, Action 71 to 84, Critical 85 to 95.
Archive track
Pending dogs, counted from registration. Watch 30 to 59 days, Action 60 to 79, Critical 80 to 95.
